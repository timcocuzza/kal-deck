# College football development checkpoint

October 26, 2026 · branch `feat/college-football`.

The College football tab now connects free public statistics, current schedules, verified Kalshi spreads, spread price history, and college strength with top-25 research evidence. It computes research from downloaded sources, not the audit's generated rankings or preview fixtures. No dependency or database-schema changes were required.

## Implemented

- Saved regular-season and postseason FBS-participant schedules, season/phase/week navigation, team logos, and dedicated matchup routes (`#cfb/game/{id}?week={week}&season_type={regular|postseason}`). FCS opponents remain visible as opponents without team statistics.
- Six facet comparisons, same/opposing-unit views, league means/medians, supporting statistics, game logs, and prominent current opponent-strength context.
- FBS-only counting by default, with an explicit option to include FBS performances against FCS. Neither scope publishes FCS rankings or opponent season metrics. This remains a research choice, not a finalized permanent counting rule.
- Published game-level EPA is labeled separately from NFL dropback/designed-run EPA. Game means receive equal weight; exact rational values determine competition ties before display rounding. Missing values remain unavailable with coverage counts.
- Five SportsDataverse tables download and validate together before replacing the single season-scoped `cfb_research:{season}` snapshot. Failed downloads or invalid identities, schema, season, flags, or numeric values retain the previous snapshot. Missing individual team-game rows produce warnings and reduced metric coverage.
- College source discovery uses `/api/sources?season=2026&sport=cfb`; default source discovery remains NFL. Reports use `/api/cfb/matchups`, `/api/cfb/rankings`, and `/api/cfb/strength`, with optional `week`, `game_id`, and `include_fcs` parameters as applicable. Source updates and errors are visible in Data and matchup disclosures.
- Refresh-on-open and manual refresh use the selected sport's sources. Switching sport/season stops the old refresh sequence after its current request and prevents stale responses from replacing the new source status.
- ESPN's current weekly scoreboard reconciles kickoff times, status, results, neutral sites, and participants against the bulk schedule. All baseline FBS-participant game IDs must be present before replacing `cfb_schedule:{season}`. TBD championship participants remain placeholders; they never receive statistics or market matches. Started, completed, and canceled games leave the upcoming list; the browser also hides confirmed games at kickoff without polling. Postponed games carry an explicit label.
- Kalshi college spreads, team identities, and game milestones download together into `cfb_markets:{season}`. The shared college series also includes FCS-only events; explicit non-FBS milestone classifications exclude these. Exact team identities, season, week, home/away, confirmed kickoff, market wording, thresholds, and settlement date must agree before a contract appears. Four reviewed team-name aliases are bound to exact provider IDs, names, division, and abbreviation. Conflicting or ambiguous matches are withheld and disclosed.
- Matchup contract selection, Yes asks before fees, settlement wording, and 7-day/24-hour/6-hour history use the existing spread interface. Home cards show the default contract and history sparkline. College history requests use `KXNCAAFSPREAD`, preserve missing hours, deduplicate downloads, and retain valid prior data after a failed refresh. Quote and history fetch times remain separate.
- Current results do not fabricate statistics: newly completed games with missing tables increase coverage expectations and generate a visible warning. The latest game with all statistics tables is displayed separately from source download times.

No college player props, injury/depth panels, blitz/coverage estimates, projected spreads, or cover probabilities were introduced.

College strength is implemented as the fourth college source, `cfb_strength`, with historical archives and current FBS/FCS results. [The rating design](cfb-elo-design.md) records the configuration and [the frozen experiment](research/cfb-elo/README.md) records its limitations. Choose **CFB → Ratings** in the header, or open `#cfb/ratings`, to compare strength, schedule difficulty, records and result evidence. Matchup cards and details open the same team evidence. FCS ratings remain internal. This supplies research for a personal top 25; manually saving an ordered ballot and written rationales is not yet implemented.

### Merge hardening

- Bookmarks optionally store `sport` and `season_type`; omitted fields retain NFL/regular behavior. CFB entries accept ESPN numeric IDs and spread contracts only. Phase-aware reads prevent regular week 1 and postseason week 1 from sharing schedule context. Watchlist notes, sides, revisions and tracker backups retain their existing storage formats.
- College market HTTP responses are cached in SQLite: quotes for 15 seconds, per-event milestones for 5 minutes, team identities for 24 hours. Cache misses are sequential and paced at least 350 ms apart. HTTP 429 uses up to three attempts with bounded exponential backoff, honors numeric/date `Retry-After`, and persists a cooldown for long limits or exhausted retries. The last coherent source snapshot remains available on failure; old cache timestamps are preserved.
- Current schedules query regular weeks and postseason weeks 1–6. The selector labels postseason as **Bowls & CFP**. All known baseline FBS game IDs must survive refresh, and completed bowl games contribute to facet rankings with the same coverage/FCS rules. Strength replay already includes postseason results.
- ESPN's [2025 postseason week 1](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/college-football/scoreboard?dates=2025&seasontype=3&week=1&groups=80&limit=500) returned 46 games, including the January 19, 2026 championship. The archived Kalshi milestone for `KXNCAAFSPREAD-26JAN19MIAIND` uses season 2025, type `PST`, round 2, and no week/division fields. Postseason contracts therefore join by season phase, exact home/away identities, an FBS participant, and confirmed kickoff; provider round numbers are not equated with ESPN weeks. All wording/threshold/date checks still apply. [Kalshi milestone API](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/milestone/get-milestones).
- Current-season bowl placeholders remain visible before team assignment, without logos for TBA identities, stats, or contracts. Exact known participants cannot be silently replaced; resolving a previously unassigned slot is allowed.
- The parser reconciled all 46 real 2025 postseason games against the SportsDataverse season schedule, including the championship at 2026-01-20 00:30 UTC. All were final; this is historical source validation, not a claim that future postseason quotes are available.
- Safari on the disposable preview verified a real CFB Watch action, No-side quote, saved notes, tracker draft handoff with exact market/game references, and return to the selected college contract. Automated tests cover retained quotes and final results, mixed NFL/CFB ticket persistence/export, phase isolation, incomplete postseason downloads, cache expiry, retry limits, and cooldown persistence.
